# Ricing 101

---

Ricing is a common activity in the Linux community. It happens mostly on Linux Distributions such as Arch and Niri among other distributions I might have not mentioned.

Why make this repo? Well this repo is for those who have the urge to make their desktop the way they want without using
other people's rices. Don't get me wrong, I have used multiple Hyprland dots, including [Caelestia Shell](https://github.com/caelestia-dots/shell), [JakooLit Dots](https://github.com/JaKooLit/Arch-Hyprland) and [DMS](https://duxlabs.dev/). They are really good dots but I kept going from one to the other since I wasn't satisfied with what they brought on the table.
At the time, I was using a 4GB Lenovo which brought constraints in Caelestia Shell and DMS which looked really good but hogged memory alot that I couldn't operate my laptop efficiently pushing me to start my own ricing journey.

Through my ricing journey, I found it hard since as it is as usual in the linux community for every person to have an opinionated look at Linux. So finding useful resources was and still is hard, unless you try to use AI and it will help you a major part but when a problem arises, you will have to burn with that fire.

I'll try to make this Repo as detailed as possible for me for future reference and for anybody who stumbles upon this and wants to start their ricing journey.

## 1. Compositor

This is the most important part in ricing since (sometimes) depeding on the compositor you are using, different ecosystems are built around it. Here is how to decide:

  - **[Hyprland](hypr.land)**: This is the most know and with the richest ecosystem. It has it's own lockscreen manager and a bunch of plugins that you might use in one way or another. It is, byy default, tiled (this means that every app you open shares the single space on the window and divides the space to fit new windows. *it can be modified to use multiple methods such as dwindle and scroll*)
    Choose it if you want full control of everything in your computer and want to use the ecosystem and want alot of references in terms of homework of other rices since it has alot of rices built behind it.

  - **[Niri](niri-wm.github.io/niri)**: This is the new kid in the block. It is a scrollable Window Manager (this means that it shows a carousel on every app you open). It has a rich ecosystem with people building rice for it.
    Choose it if you want a new taste of it or just want to do somethings different from the crowd. It's a fun experience navigating vertically and horizontally for workspaces and applications in each worksplace respectively.
